Dates are computed from a dated snapshot of published schedules (edition and verified-on date shown above); schedules get revised, so confirm current recommendations at your clinic. Catch-up for missed or delayed doses is out of scope—the chart flags a dose overdue but a doctor must plan the catch-up. Some doses are conditional (e.g. JE in endemic districts); vaxdue flags the condition but cannot decide it for your child. The official immunization card remains the record of truth.
